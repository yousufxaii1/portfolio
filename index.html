02APPROACH

Strategy without screens is theory. I do both.

"Senior product design isn't about pushing pixels faster — it's about asking better questions earlier."

I treat product design as a problem-shaping discipline first, a craft second. That means starting with the user, the data, and the business reality — and only then opening Figma.

I work with AI in my workflow — for rapid divergent exploration, design system upkeep, and the boring 30% of the job that used to eat days. The judgment, the taste, and the accountability stay with me.

Across seven years, four companies, and three industries, the through-line has been the same: clear hierarchy, honest copy, systems that scale, and interfaces that respect the user's time.

001 — DISCOVER
Research before pixels.
Strong opinions about what users want are mostly wrong. Talk to them. Watch them. Then design.
002 — SYSTEMS
Systems over screens.
A great screen is easy. A coherent product across 200 screens is the actual job.
003 — MEASURE
Ship, measure, repeat.
The first version is rarely right. The third one usually is — but only if you measure honestly.

01WHAT I DO

Five things I do well.

001
MY FAVORITE PROBLEM SPACE

AI Product Design

Designing interfaces for AI-powered products is a genuinely new design problem. Confidence levels, hallucinations, latency, prompt UX, structured output — these need their own design vocabulary. I've shipped an AI Summarization Platform end-to-end and bring that thinking to teams building generative tools, copilots, and AI-first products.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
  • Prompt & generation UX patterns
  • Streaming, loading, and confidence states
  • Output formatting and editability
  • Trust, error, and recovery flows
  • Information architecture for unstructured data
002
COMPLEX B2B / ENTERPRISE

Enterprise SaaS Design

Most B2B tools are visually cluttered, conceptually unclear, and painful to use because no one designed for the actual workflow. I specialize in making complex products feel obvious — compliance systems, financial platforms, internal tools, admin consoles.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
  • Information architecture & navigation
  • Data tables, filters, and bulk actions
  • Dashboard & analytics surfaces

  • Multi-step processes & long forms
003

End-to-End Product Design

Sometimes you don't need a specialist — you need someone who can own the entire design function. I run discovery, build the architecture, design the system, ship the screens, and partner closely with engineering through implementation.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
  • User research & concept testing
  • Information architecture & user flows
  • Wireframes, prototypes, hi-fidelity design
  • Engineering handoff & QA

02HOW I WORK

A simple process.

"Process isn't ceremony — it's the shortest path between a fuzzy brief and a shipped solution."

I tailor the depth of each phase to the project — a 2-week design sprint and a 6-month platform redesign don't deserve the same playbook. But the four phases are always present. Skipping any of them is how products end up beautiful but useless — or useful but unloved.

PHASE 01
Discover
User interviews, competitive teardowns, business goals, technical constraints. Define the actual problem before drawing anything.
PHASE 02
Design
Architecture, flows, wireframes, then high-fidelity. Multiple directions early; conviction by the end. Built on a system from day one.
PHASE 03
Validate
Prototype testing with real users, stakeholder reviews, and engineering feasibility checks. Iterate before code, not after.
PHASE 04
Ship
Detailed specs, design QA during build, post-launch usage review. Designs aren't done at handoff — they're done when they work.
